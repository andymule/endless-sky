This directory is where you should place your music files for testing with the Adaptive Music Tester.

Supported audio formats:
- MP3
- OGG
- WAV
- FLAC
- MOD

For the best testing experience, prepare several music tracks with different intensity levels. 
For example:
- ambient_theme.ogg - Calm background music
- low_intensity.ogg - Music for low-intensity gameplay
- medium_intensity.ogg - Music for medium-intensity gameplay
- high_intensity.ogg - Music for high-intensity gameplay
- boss_theme.ogg - Music for boss encounters

The Adaptive Music Tester will allow you to play these different tracks based on the intensity level
you select, and experiment with different transition types between them.
